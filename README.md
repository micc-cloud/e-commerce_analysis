# 🛍️ E-Commerce Business Intelligence & Profit Optimization

## Project Overview

Most e-commerce analysis projects focus on creating dashboards or predicting sales. While those are useful, they often stop at showing **what happened** instead of explaining **why it happened** or **what the business should do next**.

I built this project to simulate the work of a Business Analyst in a real company. Instead of treating the dataset as a Kaggle exercise, I approached it as if I had just joined an e-commerce business and was asked by management to investigate declining profitability and identify opportunities for growth.

The project follows a complete business analytics workflow, covering business understanding, data preparation, exploratory analysis, KPI design, dashboard development, and business recommendations.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Imagine you are working for an online retail company that sells products through multiple sales channels.

Although overall sales continue to grow, management has noticed several concerns:

* Profit growth is slower than revenue growth.
* Some products sell well but generate very little profit.
* Different sales channels perform very differently.
* Order cancellations and operational issues appear to be increasing.
* It is unclear which products and customers deserve more investment.

The goal of this project is to use data to answer these questions and provide recommendations that can support better business decisions.

---

# 📂 Dataset

This project uses the **Unlock Profits with E-Commerce Sales Data** dataset from Kaggle.

The dataset contains multiple business-related tables, including:

* Sales transactions
* Product information
* Pricing data
* Profit & Loss reports
* Warehouse information
* Operational data

Because the data comes from different sources, an important part of this project is integrating and transforming the data into a format suitable for business analysis.

# 📈 Project Workflow

Rather than jumping directly into visualization, I structured this project to resemble the workflow of a Business Analyst.

### 1. Business Understanding

* Define business objectives
* Understand stakeholder requirements
* Identify key business questions
* Design business KPIs

---

### 2. Data Understanding

* Explore available datasets
* Review data quality
* Build a data dictionary
* Understand relationships between tables

---

### 3. Data Preparation

* Merge multiple datasets
* Clean missing and inconsistent records
* Standardize formats
* Create analytical datasets

---

### 4. Exploratory Data Analysis

Key analyses include:

* Sales performance
* Revenue trends
* Profitability
* Product performance
* Customer behaviour
* Order status
* Operational efficiency

The objective is not only to describe the data, but also to identify patterns that could explain business performance.

---

### 5. Business Analytics

The project is divided into several business-focused modules:

* Sales Analytics
* Customer Analytics
* Product Analytics
* Pricing Analytics
* Operational Analytics
* Profitability Analytics

Each section ends with practical business insights rather than only presenting charts.

---

### 6. Dashboard Development

An interactive Power BI dashboard is developed for management, including:

* Executive Overview
* Sales Performance
* Customer Insights
* Product Performance
* Operational Performance
* Profitability Analysis

---

### 7. Business Recommendations

The final stage focuses on translating analytical findings into actionable recommendations.

Each recommendation follows a simple framework:

**Observation → Insight → Recommendation → Expected Business Impact**

This reflects how business analysts communicate with stakeholders in real organisations.
